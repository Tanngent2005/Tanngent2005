# Hi there 👋

<img align="right" width="100" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/sun0225SUN/sun0225SUN/assets/images/cxyduck.gif" alt="Coding duck" />

- 🙋 Hi, I'm **Yiyang Tan (谭逸扬)**, a senior undergraduate student majoring in Computer Science at **ShanghaiTech University**, expecting to graduate in 2027.
- 🔭 I'm currently working on **LLMs**, **AI Agents**, and **multimodal time series forecasting**, focusing on the integration of numerical signals, textual context, and agentic reasoning.
- 🌱 I'm exploring **agentic reinforcement learning**, **benchmarking & evaluation**, and **confidence-aware forecasting** — understanding when a model's predictions can be trusted.
- 📝 I'm a co-author of **[KairosAgent: Agentic Time Series Forecasting with Fused Semantic Reasoning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30002)**, accepted to **EMNLP 2026 Main Conference**. My contributions include baseline experiments, inference workflow debugging, and results and error analysis. [Project page](https://foundation-model-research.github.io/KairosAgent/)
- 🧪 I'm also interested in **time series + text benchmarks**, studying when contextual information improves forecasting and how data predictability relates to model confidence.
- 🧩 I've explored **Go backend development**, **C++ game development**, and **RISC-V** through projects and coursework, and participated in the **[GeekPie](https://geekpie.club/)** community.
- 💬 I've served as a Teaching Assistant for **CS100 (Introduction to Programming)** at ShanghaiTech, helping students with programming, assignments, and debugging.
- 📫 You can reach me at **[tanyy2023@shanghaitech.edu.cn](mailto:tanyy2023@shanghaitech.edu.cn)**.
- 🌐 You can also visit **[my blog](https://tanngent2005.github.io/)**.
